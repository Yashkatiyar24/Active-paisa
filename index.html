    APR (Annual Percentage Rate) expresses what a loan costs you over a year once the interest rate and every associated charge are counted together. Quoting it as a single percentage is what makes two competing offers genuinely comparable before you commit. Activ Paisa charges borrowers nothing directly.

    Loans arranged through Activ Paisa run from 3 to 96 months, with personal loan APRs generally falling between 10.5% and 36%. To put that in numbers: borrow ₹1,00,000 at 15% p.a. over 36 months with a 3% processing fee of ₹3,000, and the total borrowing cost comes to ₹27,795 — an APR of 17.17%.
